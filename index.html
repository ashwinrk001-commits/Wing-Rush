Privacy Policy for Wing Rush

Last updated: April 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Wing Rush is a free arcade game developed by AR Game Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

Wing Rush itself does not directly collect personal information. However, third-party services integrated into the app may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device type, OS version, and language
  • App usage and interaction data
  • Ad interaction data (clicks, views)

3. Third-Party Services – Google AdMob

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process the following to serve personalized or non-personalized ads:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device and OS information
  • Usage and interaction data
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Opt out of personalized ads: https://www.google.com/settings/ads

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Wing Rush is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through this app, please contact us at ashwinrk001@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

5. Your Privacy Rights

GDPR (EU/EEA users) – You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict or object to data processing
  • Data portability

CCPA (California users) – You have the right to:

  • Know what personal data is collected
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of sale of personal data (we do not sell data)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ashwinrk001@gmail.com.

6. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Any data collected by third-party services such as Google AdMob is retained according to their respective privacy policies.
